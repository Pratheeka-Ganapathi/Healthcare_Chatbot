# Ear, nose and throat

## Matches
ear pain, earache, ear discharge, blocked ear, hearing loss, ringing in ears, sore throat, throat pain, tonsils, blocked nose, sinus, nosebleed, hoarse voice, snoring, gale mein dard

## Questions
1. Is the problem in your ear, nose or throat, and how long has it been going on?
2. Is it on one side or both sides?
3. Do you have a fever, discharge, or difficulty swallowing?
4. On a scale of 1 to 10, how much is it bothering you?

## Red flags
- Difficulty breathing or noisy breathing
- Unable to swallow saliva
- Heavy nosebleed that does not stop
