# Skin rash and itching

## Matches
rash, itching, itchy skin, red patches, spots, acne, pimples, eczema, hives, skin allergy, dry skin, fungal infection, hair fall, nail problem

## Questions
1. Where on your body is the rash or problem, and how long has it been there?
2. Is it itchy, painful, or spreading?
3. Is this new, or has it happened before?
4. On a scale of 1 to 10, how much is it bothering you?

## Red flags
- Swelling of lips, tongue or throat
- Rash with high fever and feeling very unwell
- Blistering or peeling skin over a large area

## Likely specialty
dermatology
