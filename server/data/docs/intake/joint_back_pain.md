# Joint and back pain

## Matches
joint pain, knee pain, back pain, lower back pain, neck pain, shoulder pain, hip pain, sprain, twisted ankle, stiffness, swelling in joint, sports injury, kamar dard, ghutne mein dard

## Questions
1. Which joint or part of your back hurts, and since when?
2. Did it start after an injury, a fall, or lifting something?
3. On a scale of 1 to 10, how bad is the pain?
4. Is there swelling, or difficulty walking or moving the joint?

## Red flags
- Loss of bladder or bowel control with back pain
- Numbness or weakness in the legs
- Unable to bear any weight after a fall

## Likely specialty
orthopaedics
