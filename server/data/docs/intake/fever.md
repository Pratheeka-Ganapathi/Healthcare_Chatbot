# Fever

## Matches
fever, temperature, high temperature, feverish, chills, shivering, body heat, bukhar, jwara

## Questions
1. How many days have you had the fever?
2. Have you measured it, and how high has it gone?
3. Do you also have a cough, body ache, rash or burning while passing urine?
4. On a scale of 1 to 10, how unwell do you feel overall?

## Red flags
- Difficulty breathing
- Confusion or extreme drowsiness
- Stiff neck with fever
- Fits or seizures

## Likely specialty
general_medicine (paediatrics if under 18)
