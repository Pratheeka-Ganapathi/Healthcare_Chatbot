# Cough and cold

## Matches
cough, cold, runny nose, sneezing, chest congestion, phlegm, dry cough, wet cough, flu, khansi, zukaam, negadi

## Questions
1. How long have you had the cough or cold?
2. Is the cough dry, or are you bringing up phlegm?
3. Do you also have a fever or body ache?
4. Is it getting better, worse, or staying the same?

## Red flags
- Difficulty breathing or breathlessness at rest
- Coughing up blood
- Chest pain

## Likely specialty
general_medicine (ent if mainly nose or throat; paediatrics if under 18)
