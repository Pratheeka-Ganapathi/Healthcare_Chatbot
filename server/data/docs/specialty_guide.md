# How to choose a specialty

This guide maps common complaints to the clinic's specialties. It is a hint for routing, not a diagnosis. When a complaint is unclear or involves several body systems, choose general_medicine; the doctor can refer onward.

# general_medicine

Fever, cold, cough, body ache, fatigue, infections, stomach upset, headache, diabetes, blood pressure, thyroid, general health check-ups, and any adult complaint that does not clearly belong to another specialty.

# dermatology

Skin rashes, itching, acne, eczema, psoriasis, fungal skin infections, hair loss, dandruff, nail problems, moles and skin allergies.

# orthopaedics

Joint pain, knee pain, back pain, neck pain, shoulder pain, sprains, sports injuries, fractures, arthritis and bone problems.

# paediatrics

Any health concern in a patient younger than 18 years, including fever, cough, colds, vaccinations and growth check-ups. Adults aged 18 or more are not seen in paediatrics.

# gynaecology

Women's health: periods, menstrual pain, pregnancy, fertility, menopause, vaginal discharge, breast concerns and preventive screening such as pap smears.

# ent

Ear pain, ear discharge, hearing problems, ringing in the ears, blocked nose, sinus problems, nosebleeds, sore throat, tonsillitis, voice problems and snoring.
