# Consultations

City Care Clinic offers outpatient consultations in general medicine, dermatology, orthopaedics, paediatrics, gynaecology and ENT. Consultations are by appointment.

# Lab tests

Our in-house lab collects samples for common blood tests including complete blood count, blood sugar (fasting and post-meal), HbA1c, lipid profile, thyroid profile, liver and kidney function tests, and urine routine. Sample collection runs Monday to Saturday from 8:00 AM to 11:00 AM. Most reports are ready the same evening.

# Imaging

The clinic does not have X-ray, ultrasound, CT or MRI on site. Your doctor can refer you to a partner diagnostic centre nearby.

# ECG

A resting ECG can be done at the clinic during clinic hours when your doctor requests it.

# Vaccinations

Childhood vaccinations are given by the paediatrics team on appointment. Adult flu vaccination is available seasonally; ask the front desk about availability.

# Minor procedures

Minor procedures such as dressing of small wounds, ear wax removal and suture removal are done on appointment after a consultation.

# Health check-up packages

Basic health check-up packages include a general physician consultation and a set of blood tests. Ask the front desk for the current list of packages.

# Reports and records

Lab reports can be collected from the front desk. Bring a photo ID to collect reports.
