# About City Care Clinic

City Care Clinic is a multi-specialty outpatient clinic in Bangalore. This is a demo clinic with synthetic data. We offer consultations in general medicine, dermatology, orthopaedics, paediatrics, gynaecology and ENT, along with basic lab tests and minor procedures.

# Address and directions

City Care Clinic, 2nd Floor, 14 Demo Main Road, Indiranagar, Bangalore 560038. The clinic is a 5-minute walk from Indiranagar metro station. Auto and cab drop-off is at the main gate.

# Parking

Two-wheeler parking is available in the building basement. Car parking is limited to 6 spaces on a first-come basis. Paid public parking is available on 100 Feet Road.

# Timing

Clinic timing and opening hours: The clinic is open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM to 8:30 PM. The clinic is closed on Sundays and on announced holidays. Each doctor has their own consulting days and hours; ask me about a specific doctor to see their schedule.

# Contact

Front desk phone: 080 0000 1234, Monday to Saturday during clinic hours. For anything this assistant cannot help with, the front desk team can assist.

# Emergencies

City Care Clinic is an outpatient clinic and does not have an emergency department. In a medical emergency call 108 for an ambulance or 112 for the national emergency number, or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

# New patients and registration

New patients can register through this assistant with their full name, mobile number, date of birth and sex, and then book straight away. You can also register at the front desk on your first visit; please arrive 15 minutes early if you do.

# What to bring

Please bring a photo ID, your previous prescriptions and any recent test reports. If you have health insurance, bring your insurance card or policy number.

# Payment

We accept cash, UPI, and debit and credit cards. Consultation fees are paid at the front desk before the consultation.

# Arriving for your appointment

Please arrive 10 minutes before your appointment time. If you are more than 15 minutes late, the front desk may need to move you to the next available slot.

# Cancellation policy

Appointments can be cancelled up to 2 hours before the appointment time through this assistant. For later changes, please call the front desk.

# Follow-up visits

Some visits are charged at a reduced follow-up fee. Your doctor decides whether a visit counts as a follow-up. The follow-up fee applies only when the doctor has marked your visit for follow-up and you are coming back for the same issue.
