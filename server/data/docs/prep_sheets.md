# Fasting blood sugar test preparation

For a fasting blood sugar test, do not eat or drink anything except plain water for 8 to 10 hours before the sample. Come to the lab between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM. For questions about your medicines before the test, please ask your doctor.

# Lipid profile test preparation

For a lipid profile, fast for 10 to 12 hours before the sample. Plain water is allowed. Avoid heavy or fatty meals the evening before.

# HbA1c test preparation

An HbA1c test does not need fasting. You can come for the sample at any time during lab hours.

# Thyroid test preparation

A thyroid profile does not need fasting. Coming in the morning is preferred so repeat tests are taken at a similar time of day. For questions about your medicines before the test, please ask your doctor.

# Urine test preparation

For a urine routine test, collect a midstream sample in the sterile container from the clinic, preferably the first sample of the morning.

# Preparing for a general medicine consultation

Bring previous prescriptions, any recent test reports, and a list of your symptoms and when they started.

# Preparing for a dermatology consultation

Avoid applying creams, makeup or nail polish on the affected area on the day of the visit. Bring photos if the rash comes and goes.

# Preparing for an orthopaedics consultation

Wear loose clothing so the affected joint can be examined. Bring any previous X-ray or scan reports and films.

# Preparing for a paediatrics consultation

Bring the child's vaccination card and growth records. A parent or guardian must accompany children under 18.

# Preparing for a gynaecology consultation

Note the date of your last menstrual period. Bring any previous scan or test reports.

# Preparing for an ENT consultation

Bring any previous hearing test reports. Avoid cleaning your ears with buds before the visit.
