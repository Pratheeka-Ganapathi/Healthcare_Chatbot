# Insurance accepted

City Care Clinic is a demo clinic. For this demo, the clinic accepts cashless outpatient claims from Star Health, HDFC ERGO, ICICI Lombard and Niva Bupa where the policy includes outpatient cover.

# Cashless outpatient claims

For cashless outpatient claims, show your insurance card and a photo ID at the front desk before the consultation. The front desk checks your eligibility with the insurer, which can take 15 to 30 minutes.

# Reimbursement claims

If your insurer is not on our cashless list, pay at the front desk and ask for an itemised bill and the consultation note. You can submit these to your insurer for reimbursement.

# Corporate and employer plans

Employees covered under corporate plans with the listed insurers can use the same cashless process. Please bring your employee ID along with your insurance card.

# Insurance questions

The front desk at 080 0000 1234 can confirm whether a specific policy or plan is accepted.
